ShentuBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShentuBot ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how ShentuBot collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using ShentuBot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Service.

2. Information We Collect

ShentuBot collects the following types of information:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you connect your TikTok account to ShentuBot, we receive your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture as authorized by you through the TikTok API.
  • Content Data: Videos, images, text, and other content you create or upload through ShentuBot for publishing to TikTok.
  • Communication Data: Any information you provide when contacting our support team, including email addresses and message content.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use ShentuBot, including features accessed, actions taken, time spent, and interaction patterns.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, screen resolution, and unique device identifiers.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed, and referring URLs collected automatically when you use the Service.

2.3 Information from TikTok API

  • Authorization Tokens: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to publish content on your behalf.
  • Profile Information: Basic profile data as permitted by the scopes you authorize, including your TikTok user ID and username.
  • Publishing Status: Information about the status of content published through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Creator Information: Creator account status and capabilities, queried before publishing to verify posting permissions.

3. How We Use Your Information

ShentuBot uses the information we collect for the following purposes:

  1. Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve the core functionality of ShentuBot, including publishing content to your TikTok account as authorized by you.
  2. Authentication: To verify your identity and manage your authorized connection to TikTok.
  3. Communication: To respond to your inquiries, provide technical support, and send you important notifications about the Service.
  4. Analytics and Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and improve the performance and user experience of ShentuBot.
  5. Security: To detect, prevent, and address fraud, unauthorized access, and other illegal activities, and to protect the security of our Service and users.
  6. Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

ShentuBot does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With TikTok: We share content and authorization data with TikTok as necessary to publish content on your behalf through the TikTok Content Posting API. This sharing is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy and the permissions you grant.
  • Service Providers: We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Service, provided they are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to continued protection under this Privacy Policy.

4.1 AI-Generated Content

ShentuBot uses artificial intelligence to generate video content for publishing. Please be aware of the following:

  • Content Generation: ShentuBot may use AI services to generate video content based on user-provided prompts or task requirements. The AI-generated content is reviewed by the user before publishing.
  • Content Ownership: Users retain full ownership and responsibility for all content published through ShentuBot, including AI-generated content.
  • Content Compliance: All published content must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. ShentuBot does not guarantee the appropriateness of AI-generated content and users are responsible for reviewing content before publication.
  • No Automated Account Operation: ShentuBot does not operate user accounts in an automated manner. Each publication requires explicit user authorization through the OAuth flow and manual confirmation before publishing.

4.2 Content Posting Process

ShentuBot utilizes the TikTok Content Posting API to publish content. The process involves:

  • User Authorization: Users must explicitly authorize ShentuBot through TikTok's OAuth 2.0 flow, granting specific permissions (scopes) for content publishing.
  • Content Review: Before publishing, users can review and modify all content including titles, descriptions, privacy settings, and video content.
  • Direct Posting: Content is published directly to the user's TikTok account using the authorized API credentials. ShentuBot does not store or distribute content beyond what is necessary for the publishing process.
  • Transparency: All content published through ShentuBot is clearly identifiable as originating from the user's authorized actions.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers with appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

5.2 Security Measures

ShentuBot implements industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Secure storage of OAuth tokens using encryption at rest.
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing.
  • Access controls limiting employee access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification: The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: The right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Data Portability: The right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: The right to withdraw your consent for data processing at any time by disconnecting your TikTok account from ShentuBot.
  • Objection: The right to object to certain types of processing of your personal information.
